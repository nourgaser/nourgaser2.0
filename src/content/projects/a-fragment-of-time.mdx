A Fragment of Time is a pixel art step-based strategy roguelike set on a
hexagonal grid. You play as a lost soul who can manipulate time to defeat enemies
and solve puzzles — but spacetime is fragile. Use your powers irresponsibly and
things break.

The game features a custom hexagonal tile system, an Aseprite hot-reload
pipeline for live pixel art iteration, and a turn-based mechanic built around
time manipulation as a core game verb.
