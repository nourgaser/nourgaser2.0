Charged Learning is an educational game about electricity and circuits, built as
my bachelor's degree graduation project. Players explore interactive circuit
simulations, learning the fundamentals of electronics through hands-on gameplay
rather than passive reading. The game was designed with a full Figma prototype
before implementation in Unity.
