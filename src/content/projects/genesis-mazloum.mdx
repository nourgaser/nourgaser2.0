A production e-commerce website built for Mazloum Tiles to present products and categories clearly and support customer purchase journeys. The platform serves a broad home and finishing-products catalog across tiles, bathroom, and kitchen units, with a focus on clarity, discoverability, and a smooth shopping experience.

Alongside the storefront, a separate layer of automation and sync tooling connects the online catalog and orders with the offline point-of-sale and inventory system used across Mazloum Tiles' physical stores and storage units, keeping online and in-store inventory in agreement.
