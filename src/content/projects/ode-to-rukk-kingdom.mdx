A 2D PvP strategy game where different Ork tribes compete for the establishment
of the Rukk Kingdom. Choose a tribe, build your forces, and lead them to victory.

The project is currently in active development — the playable demo showcases
keyboard and mouse grid navigation with a control-hijacking mechanic that lets
players influence unit pathfinding mid-move.
