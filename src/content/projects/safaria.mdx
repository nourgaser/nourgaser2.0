Safaria is a digital travel marketplace connecting travelers with trusted Saudi travel agencies, letting users book whole packages — flights, hotels, transportation, and tours — through one app instead of stitching a trip together across agencies. Partner travel agencies can create and list their own tour offerings on the platform, and travelers get upfront package pricing, reviews, and direct chat with agencies.

This was ANY Solutions' first client engagement. I architected and led the web, cloud, and infrastructure side of the platform, and managed the external Flutter developers who built the iOS and Android apps.
